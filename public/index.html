📖 Supported ISL Vocabulary (10 Signs) Standard ISL Reference
Hello1 Hand
Open palm raised near temple, angled outward in polite salute/wave.
Namaste2 Hands
Both palms pressed flat together at center chest (Anjali Mudra).
Thank You1 Hand
Flat hand fingertips at chin moving forward toward listener.
Yes1 Hand
Closed fist held forward nodding downward from wrist.
No1 Hand
Index finger upright waving side-to-side; other fingers curled.
Please1 Hand
Open flat palm gently resting over heart/chest.
Help2 Hands
Thumbs-up fist resting upright on the flat open palm of other hand.
Good1 Hand
Clear thumbs-up gesture held firmly at chest level.
Water1 Hand
'W' shape with index, middle, ring upright; tapped at lips.
Goodbye1 Hand
Open palm facing forward, waving side-to-side in farewell.
⚠️ Prototype Notice: SignBridge is a limited-vocabulary assistive communication prototype developed for hackathon demonstration. Joining sign glosses provides functional visual and audio aid, but does not represent full grammatical translation of complex conversational ISL.
